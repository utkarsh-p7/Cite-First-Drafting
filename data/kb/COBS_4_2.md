## Applies to (scope)
Communications and financial promotions to clients. Applies whether the client is retail or professional, taking account of the nature of the client.

## What the firm must do (paraphrase)
- Ensure a communication or financial promotion is **fair, clear and not misleading**.

## Material quote (<15 words)
"Must ensure that a communication or financial promotion is fair, clear and not misleading to the client" - COBS 4.2.1.R

## Relevance to tool / ethical boundary
A suitability report is a client communication → must be fair, clear, not misleading. Under Consumer Duty this is reinforced by **PRIN 2A.5 (consumer understanding)**. Direct support for the tool's plain-English check.
