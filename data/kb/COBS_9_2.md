## Applies to (scope)
Non-MiFID suitability assessment. Analogue to 9A.2 for the deferred non-MiFID products.

## What the firm must do (paraphrase)
- Obtain the information necessary to understand the essential facts and have a **reasonable basis** for believing the transaction: meets the client's investment objectives (including that the client can **financially bear the related risks**); and that the client has the **necessary experience and knowledge** to understand the risks.
- Investment-objectives information: length of holding period, risk preferences, risk profile and purpose.
- Financial-situation information: source/extent of regular income, assets (incl. liquid), regular commitments.
- The firm **must not encourage a client not to provide information** for the suitability assessment.
- The firm may rely on client-provided information unless it is manifestly out of date, inaccurate or incomplete.
- Keep a record for **five years**.

## Material quote (<15 words)
"A firm must not encourage a client not to provide information" — COBS 9.2.
