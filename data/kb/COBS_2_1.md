## Applies to (scope)
Overarching conduct standard. Applies to designated investment business for retail clients; MiFID/etc. for any client; insurance distribution for any client. [Note: art 24(1) MiFID]

## What the firm must do (paraphrase)
- Act honestly, fairly and professionally in line with the best interests of the client (the **client's best interests rule**).
- This obligation is treated as **including** the duties under Principles 1 (integrity), 2 (skill, care & diligence) and 6 (customers' interests).

## Material quote (<15 words)
"act honestly, fairly and professionally in accordance with the best interests of its client" — COBS 2.1.1R.

## Relevance to tool / ethical boundary
Frames the whole tool: a drafted report must serve the client's interest and not mislead. **Principle 6 is superseded by Principle 12 (Consumer Duty) where the Duty applies** — see PRIN_2A / FG22_5.
