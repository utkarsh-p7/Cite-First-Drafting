## Applies to (scope)
Guidance on how firms comply with Principle 12 + PRIN 2A. Non-binding; does not replace the Handbook (¶1.11). For investments, the Duty applies to **non-professional clients** per COBS (¶2.3).

## What it requires / explains (paraphrase)
- Duty components: Consumer Principle + three cross-cutting rules + four outcomes (products & services; price & value; consumer understanding; consumer support) (¶1.3).
- **Reasonableness** is an objective test — the standard of a prudent firm carrying on the same activity with understanding of the target market (¶1.4).
- "must / should / may" convention defined (¶1.15).
- Chapter map: Ch.7 = price & value; **Ch.8 = consumer understanding** (communications that equip customers to make effective, timely, informed decisions); Ch.9 = consumer support.

## Material quote (<15 words)
"sets the standard of care that firms should give to customers in retail financial markets" — ¶1.1.

## Relevance to tool / ethical boundary
- **Ch.8 (consumer understanding)** = direct authority for the plain-English evaluation criterion on the report.
- **Ch.7 (price & value)** = authority for the costs / fair-value content.
- Confirms proportionality for a **micro-firm** (¶1.6): same standard, capability scaled to size — supports the lightweight-tool framing.
