## Applies to (scope)
Suitability reports for **non-MiFID / non-IBIP** business (e.g. life policy, personal/stakeholder pension). **Mostly out of v1 scope** (ISA+GIA are MiFID). Retained only for the **non-MiFID logic hook** and as comparator; becomes live if **personal pension** is admitted to v1 (currently DEFERRED).

## What the report must contain / do (paraphrase)
- Explain why the firm concluded the recommended transaction is suitable, **having regard to the information the client provided**.
- (Life policy) include a personalised recommendation explaining why that policy best meets the client's demands and needs. [Note: art 20(1) IDD]
- Detail must be **modulated by the complexity of the transaction and the type of client**.
- 9.4.11R: disclose additional charges — including initial product charges and charges for accessing existing funds or moving funds to a different scheme.

## Material quote (<15 words)
"explain why the firm has concluded that the recommended transaction is suitable" — COBS 9.4.7R.

## Relevance to tool / ethical boundary
Fallback regime only. If pension is later included, the profile must branch to 9.4/9.2 logic. Same boundary: explain the suitability conclusion; never generate it.
