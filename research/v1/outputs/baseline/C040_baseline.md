## Why this recommendation is suitable for you

The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is a multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities, 40 % bonds) that aims for capital growth over a period of five years or more. Although the fund’s recommended minimum holding period is five years, its balanced risk profile (summary risk indicator 4 of 7) aligns with your balanced attitude to risk and willingness to accept some market fluctuation for the potential of higher growth.  

Holding the fund inside a Stocks & Shares ISA means any income or capital gains are free from UK income tax and capital gains tax, which supports your goal of maximising the amount saved for university costs. The ISA also offers daily dealing and relatively quick withdrawals (normally within five business days) should you need to access the money before the five‑year horizon.

Your existing cash assets (£34,000) and the absence of liabilities give you a strong capacity to absorb short‑term market movements, consistent with the fund’s risk level.

## Risks and possible disadvantages

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount you invest, especially in a downturn, because there is no capital guarantee.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the portfolio to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **Time horizon mismatch:** The fund is designed for a minimum holding period of five years, whereas your stated horizon is about three years. Exiting the investment earlier could mean you experience volatility and potentially lower returns than expected.  
- **ISA flexibility:** The ISA is not a flexible ISA; any amount withdrawn cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your remaining ISA allowance.
