## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

- **Alignment with objective and horizon:** The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is designed for capital growth over a period of five years or more, matching your 5‑year target horizon for the career‑break fund.  
- **Risk profile match:** The fund’s risk indicator is 4 of 7, which corresponds to your “moderately adventurous” risk attitude.  
- **Capacity for loss:** Your high capacity for loss and substantial liquid assets mean you can tolerate the market swings you indicated you are comfortable with.  
- **Tax efficiency:** As a Stocks & Shares ISA, any income or capital gains generated within the fund are free from UK income tax and capital gains tax, which supports the goal of maximising the amount available for your career break.  
- **Cost‑effectiveness:** Total annual charges of 0.85 % (0.25 % platform fee + 0.60 % fund OCF) are transparent and there are no entry, exit or performance fees, helping more of your £14,500 to stay invested.  
- **Liquidity:** Daily dealing and withdrawals typically processed within five business days give you access to the money if your circumstances change, while the recommended minimum holding period of five years aligns with your investment horizon.  

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

- **Market risk:** The value of the fund can fall below the amount you invest, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back the £14,500 you invest.  
- **ISA flexibility:** The ISA is not a flexible ISA; any amount withdrawn cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.  

## Costs and charges  

- Platform fee: 0.25 % per annum.  
- Fund ongoing charge (OCF): 0.60 % per annum.  
- Total illustrative annual cost: 0.85 % of the investment value.  
- No entry, exit or performance fees apply.
