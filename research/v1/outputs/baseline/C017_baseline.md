## The advice we are giving you

We recommend that you invest the £26,000 inheritance as a lump‑sum into a Stocks & Shares ISA (SS ISA). The ISA will hold the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund, a diversified multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds).  

## Your objectives, circumstances, knowledge and experience

- **Objective:** You want to invest the inheritance for growth over a horizon of around 7 years.  
- **Age & employment:** 64 years old, self‑employed, earning a gross annual salary of £115,900.  
- **Financial situation:**  
  - Monthly essential expenditure £1,350 and discretionary expenditure £1,100.  
  - Cash savings of £37,500 and no existing investments, property or liabilities.  
  - Emergency‑fund coverage of 27.8 months, giving you a high capacity for loss.  
  - ISA allowance already used £10,000, leaving further allowance available.  
- **Knowledge & experience:** You have previously held cash savings, funds and shares, but trade only rarely. You are a relevant professional and therefore have a good understanding of investment products.  

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you

- **Risk tolerance & capacity:** You described yourself as “adventurous”, willing to take significant risk for higher returns, and you have a high capacity for loss supported by substantial liquid assets and high disposable income. The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund carries a risk indicator of 4 / 7, matching an adventurous risk profile while still providing diversification.  
- **Investment horizon:** The fund’s policy is to achieve capital growth over 5 + years, which aligns with your 7‑year horizon and the recommended minimum holding period of 5 years.  
- **Tax efficiency:** As a Stocks & Shares ISA, any income or capital gains generated within the fund are free from UK income tax and CGT, which is appropriate for an inheritance you wish to grow.  
- **Liquidity:** Daily dealing and withdrawals within five business days give you access to the money if needed, while the non‑flexible ISA rules are acceptable given your long‑term growth aim.  
- **Cost‑effectiveness:** Total annual charges of approximately 0.85 % (platform fee 0.25 % + fund OCF 0.60 %) are low for a diversified multi‑asset fund, supporting good value for your investment.  

## Risks and possible disadvantages

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back the original £26,000.  
- **ISA withdrawal limits:** Once you withdraw, you cannot replace the amount within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.  

## Costs and charges

- Platform fee: 0.25 % per annum.  
- Fund ongoing charge (OCF): 0.60 % per annum.  
- No entry, exit or performance fees.  
- Illustrative total annual cost: 0.85 % of the investment value.  

## Ongoing review of your arrangements

Given your adventurous risk profile, the 7‑year horizon and the nature of the multi‑asset fund, a periodic review (at least annually) is likely to be needed to ensure the investment remains appropriate as market conditions and your personal circumstances evolve.
