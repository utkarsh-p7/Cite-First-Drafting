## The advice we are giving you  

We recommend that you invest £18,000 of your available cash into a Stocks & Shares ISA (the “ISA”) and make regular monthly contributions into the same ISA. The ISA will hold the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund, a multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds).

- **Primary objective:** General wealth accumulation.  
- **Target amount:** £40,000.  
- **Investment horizon:** Around 5 years.  
- **Age:** 37.  
- **Employment:** Employed, salary of £37,000 per year.  
- **Dependants:** 2.  
- **Tax band:** Basic rate.  
- **Financial situation:**  
  - Monthly essential expenditure £1,750 and discretionary expenditure £750.  
  - Cash savings £14,500 and existing investments £11,500.  
  - No liabilities and an emergency fund covering 8.3 months of expenses.  
  - Capacity for loss: Medium, based on a stable income, adequate emergency fund and a moderate surplus.  
- **Knowledge and experience:** You have previously held cash savings, funds and shares and trade occasionally. You have relevant professional experience.  
- **Risk attitude:** Adventurous (ATR score 5) and you are happy to take significant risk for higher returns.  

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

The Stocks & Shares ISA holding the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is designed for capital growth over a period of five years or more, matching your 5‑year horizon and wealth‑accumulation goal. The fund’s diversified multi‑asset allocation aligns with your adventurous risk tolerance while still providing some bond exposure, which is consistent with your medium capacity for loss.  

Because the ISA wrapper shelters income and capital gains from UK income tax and CGT, it makes efficient use of your remaining ISA allowance (£12,500 already used). The recommended regular monthly contributions allow you to build the investment gradually, fitting with your monthly discretionary cash flow.  

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose you to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back the £18,000 you invest.  
- **Liquidity:** While you can withdraw daily, the ISA is not flexible – any amount withdrawn cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your ISA allowance.  
- **Holding period:** The provider recommends a minimum holding period of five years; exiting earlier may increase the chance of loss.  

## Costs and charges  

- Platform fee: 0.25 % per annum.  
- Fund ongoing charge (OCF): 0.60 % per annum.  
- No entry, exit or performance fees.  
- **Illustrative total annual cost:** 0.85 % of the investment value.  

## Ongoing review of your arrangements  

Given the five‑year investment horizon and the market‑linked nature of the fund, a periodic review (at least annually) is likely to be needed to ensure the investment remains appropriate for your circumstances and objectives.
