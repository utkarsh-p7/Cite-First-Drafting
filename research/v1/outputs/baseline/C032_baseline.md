## Why this recommendation is suitable for you

The Stocks & Shares ISA with the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is designed for capital growth over a period of five years or more, matching your 15‑year horizon. The fund’s diversified multi‑asset approach (approximately 60 % equities, 40 % bonds) aligns with a moderately cautious risk profile (risk indicator 4 of 7) and your stated willingness to tolerate modest short‑term fluctuations.  

Because the ISA wrapper shelters income and capital gains from UK income tax and CGT, it helps you retain more of any growth toward your £140,000 house‑deposit goal. The product has no entry, exit or performance fees, and the total annual cost (platform fee 0.25 % p.a. plus fund OCF 0.60 % p.a.) is 0.85 % p.a., which is reasonable for a retail investment of this size.  

Your existing cash savings (£53,000) and the £5,500 you wish to invest give you a comfortable emergency fund and surplus, supporting the “medium” capacity for loss and the ability to stay invested for the recommended minimum holding period of five years.

## Risks and possible disadvantages

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a market downturn.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose you to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back the original £5,500.  
- **Liquidity:** While you can withdraw daily, the ISA is not flexible – any amount withdrawn cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.  
- **Holding period:** The product is intended for a minimum of five years; exiting earlier may expose you to higher short‑term volatility.
