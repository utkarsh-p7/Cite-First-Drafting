## Why this recommendation is suitable for you

The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund aims for capital growth over a period of five years or more, matching your 7‑year horizon. Although the fund carries a moderate risk level (summary risk indicator 4 of 7), it is diversified across equities and bonds, which helps to smooth volatility while still offering growth potential.  

Your cautious risk attitude is balanced by a high capacity for loss, meaning you can tolerate short‑term market fluctuations without jeopardising your day‑to‑day finances. The ISA wrapper provides tax‑free treatment of any income and capital gains, which aligns with your higher‑rate tax position and the goal of maximising the amount available for university costs.  

The minimum investment requirement (£1,000 lump sum) is well below your available £14,000, and the fund has no entry, exit, or performance fees, keeping costs transparent.

## Risks and possible disadvantages

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to exchange‑rate movements.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no protection of your capital; losses are possible.  
- **Liquidity:** While you can withdraw daily, the ISA is not flexible – any withdrawn amount cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your ISA allowance.  
- **Risk level:** The fund’s risk indicator of 4/7 may be higher than you expect for a “cautious” investor, so you should be comfortable with moderate volatility.
