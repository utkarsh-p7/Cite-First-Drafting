## The advice we are giving you  

We recommend that you invest the £32,500 you have available in a diversified multi‑asset fund (the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund).  
To make the most of tax advantages and flexibility, we suggest using **both** a Stocks & Shares ISA wrapper and an unwrapped General Investment Account (GIA) wrapper. The fund will be held in each wrapper in the same retail share class.

## Your objectives, circumstances, knowledge and experience  

* **Primary objective:** Save for your two children’s university costs.  
* **Target amount:** £64,000 in about 15 years.  
* **Risk attitude:** Cautious (ATR score 1). You have expressed that you do not want to see the investment value fall noticeably in a bad year.  
* **Financial situation:**  
  * Gross annual income £7,600 (part‑time earnings).  
  * Monthly essential outgoings £900 and discretionary outgoings £1,500.  
  * Cash savings £46,500 and existing investments £25,000, giving an emergency fund of roughly 52 months.  
  * Liabilities £4,500.  
  * Capacity for loss is “medium”, based on a stable income, adequate emergency fund and a moderate surplus.  
* **Knowledge & experience:** You have previously held cash savings and a pension and trade only rarely.  

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

* **Investment term:** The fund’s policy is capital growth over a period of 5 years or more, matching your 15‑year horizon.  
* **Risk profile:** The fund carries a summary risk indicator of 4 out of 7 – a moderate risk level that aligns with your cautious attitude while still offering growth potential to reach the £64,000 target.  
* **Capacity for loss:** Your medium capacity for loss, supported by a large cash buffer and stable part‑time income, means you can tolerate the moderate market fluctuations associated with a 4/7 risk rating.  
* **Tax efficiency:** Holding part of the investment in a Stocks & Shares ISA means any income and capital gains are free from UK income tax and CGT, helping you preserve more of the fund’s growth for the university goal.  
* **Flexibility:** The GIA component provides unrestricted access and the ability to withdraw funds before the ISA allowance is exhausted, giving you liquidity if needed.  
* **Cost‑effectiveness:** Both wrappers use the same platform and fund, resulting in a total annual charge of approximately 0.85 % (0.25 % platform fee + 0.60 % fund OCF), which is reasonable for a diversified multi‑asset fund.  

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

* **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a market downturn. This could be uncomfortable given your desire to avoid noticeable drops.  
* **Currency risk:** A portion of the fund is invested in non‑GBP assets, exposing you to exchange‑rate movements.  
* **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back the original £32,500.  
* **ISA withdrawal limits:** Withdrawals from the ISA cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance, which may limit future contributions if you need to top‑up later.
