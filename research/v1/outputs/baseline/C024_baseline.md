## Why this recommendation is suitable for you
The SS ISA holding the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund matches your moderate‑adventurous risk profile (risk indicator 4 of 7) and your long‑term horizon of around 20 years. The fund’s objective of capital growth over 5 + years aligns with your goal of building a sizeable university fund.  

Your high capacity for loss and substantial liquid assets mean you can absorb market fluctuations without jeopardising day‑to‑day finances. The ISA wrapper provides tax‑free growth, which is beneficial given your additional‑rate tax band. The minimum investment of £1,000 is well below your available £18,500, allowing the full amount to be invested immediately as a lump sum.

## Risks and possible disadvantages
- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in periods of market stress.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to exchange‑rate movements.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back the original £18,500.  
- **Liquidity constraints:** While you can withdraw daily, the ISA is not flexible – any amount withdrawn cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.  
- **Minimum holding period:** The product is recommended for a minimum of 5 years; exiting earlier may expose you to short‑term market volatility.
