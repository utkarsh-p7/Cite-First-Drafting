## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

The Stocks & Shares ISA holds a diversified multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds) whose investment objective is capital growth over a period of five years or more. This matches your 5‑year horizon and wealth‑accumulation goal.  

Your high capacity for loss and the fact that you have a sizeable cash buffer mean you can tolerate the market risk associated with a fund that has a risk indicator of 4 / 7. The ISA wrapper provides tax‑free treatment of income and capital gains, which is beneficial given your higher‑rate tax band.  

The recommended minimum investment (£1,000 lump sum or £50 per month) is compatible with the £29,000 you wish to invest.

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no protection of your capital if the market falls.  
- **Liquidity:** While daily dealing is available, withdrawals are not replaceable within the same tax year because the ISA is not flexible.
