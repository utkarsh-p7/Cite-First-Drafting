## Why this recommendation is suitable for you
The Stocks & Shares ISA holding the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund matches your stated objective of capital growth over a 5‑plus‑year period and is held within a tax‑efficient wrapper (ISA) that shelters income and gains from UK tax. The fund’s risk indicator is 4 of 7, which aligns with a cautious investor who is willing to accept some market fluctuation but prefers a diversified portfolio rather than a pure cash product. Your medium capacity for loss, stable income and solid emergency fund support the suitability of a balanced multi‑asset approach. The recommended lump‑sum investment of £36,000 fits within your available investable amount and uses the ISA allowance that is currently unused.

## Risks and possible disadvantages
- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a market downturn.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that the original £36,000 will be preserved.  
- **Liquidity:** While daily dealing is available, withdrawals are not flexible within the same tax year; withdrawn amounts cannot be replaced without using your ISA allowance.  
- **Holding period:** The product is recommended for a minimum of 5 years; exiting earlier may increase the chance of loss.
