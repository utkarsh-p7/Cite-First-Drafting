## The advice we are giving you  

We recommend that you invest the £28,500 you have available as a lump‑sum into a Stocks & Shares ISA (the “ss_isa”). The ISA will hold the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund, a multi‑asset fund with an approximate 60 % allocation to global equities and 40 % to bonds.

- **Primary objective:** Build a house deposit of £47,000.  
- **Time horizon:** Around 20 years.  
- **Age:** 49.  
- **Employment:** Self‑employed, earning a gross annual salary of £35,800.  
- **Dependants:** 2.  
- **Financial situation:**  
  - Monthly essential expenditure £2,100 and discretionary expenditure £1,050.  
  - Cash savings of £27,000 and no existing investments, property or liabilities.  
  - Emergency fund covers approximately 12.9 months of outgoings.  
  - Capacity for loss is assessed as “medium”, based on a stable income, adequate emergency fund and a moderate surplus.  
- **Tax position:** Basic‑rate taxpayer.  
- **ISA allowance used:** £7,000 of the current year’s allowance has already been used.  
- **Knowledge and experience:** You have previously held cash savings, funds and shares but do not trade. You have never traded before.  

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

- **Risk tolerance:** Your attitude to risk is “moderately adventurous” (ATR score 4) and you are comfortable with meaningful swings in value. The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund carries a risk indicator of 4 / 7, matching your risk profile.  
- **Capacity for loss:** Your medium capacity for loss, supported by a stable income and a sizable emergency fund, aligns with the fund’s market risk (value can fall below the amount invested).  
- **Investment horizon:** The fund is designed for capital growth over a period of 5 + years; your 20‑year horizon comfortably exceeds the recommended minimum holding period.  
- **Tax efficiency:** As a Stocks & Shares ISA, any income and capital gains generated are free from UK income tax and CGT, which is beneficial given your basic‑rate status.  
- **Liquidity:** Daily dealing and withdrawals within five business days give you access to the money if needed, while the non‑flexible nature of the ISA is acceptable because you do not intend to replace withdrawals within the same tax year.  
- **Cost‑effectiveness:** The total illustrative charge of 0.85 % p.a. (platform fee 0.25 % p.a. plus fund OCF 0.60 % p.a.) is competitive for a diversified multi‑asset fund.  

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall, especially in a bad market year, and there is no capital guarantee.  
- **Currency risk:** Part of the portfolio is invested in non‑GBP assets, exposing you to exchange‑rate movements.  
- **Liquidity limitation:** Withdrawn amounts cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your remaining ISA allowance.  
- **Long‑term commitment:** Although you can withdraw at any time, the fund is intended for a minimum of five years to achieve its growth objective; early withdrawals may affect the achievement of your house‑deposit target.  

## Costs and charges  

- Platform fee: 0.25 % per annum.  
- Fund ongoing charge (OCF): 0.60 % per annum.  
- No entry, exit or performance fees.  
- Illustrative total annual cost: 0.85 % of the investment value.
