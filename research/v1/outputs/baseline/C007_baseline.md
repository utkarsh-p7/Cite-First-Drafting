## Why this recommendation is suitable for you

The SS ISA holds the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund, a diversified multi‑asset portfolio (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds) that aims for capital growth over a period of five years or more. This matches your long‑term horizon of about 20 years and your desire for growth to fund a future house deposit.

The risk indicator for the fund is 4 / 7, which aligns with your moderately cautious attitude – you are comfortable with modest market fluctuations but not with large, sudden losses. The fund’s policy of no capital guarantee is consistent with your low capacity for loss, as you have indicated you can tolerate only small dips and have a limited surplus to absorb losses.

Using an ISA wrapper gives you tax‑free growth – any income or capital gains generated within the ISA are free from UK income tax and CGT, which supports your objective of maximising the amount saved for a house deposit.

The recommended regular monthly contribution fits with your cash flow, allowing you to invest gradually while maintaining your essential and discretionary spending.

## Risks and possible disadvantages

- **Market risk:** The value of the fund can fall below the amount you invest, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Part of the portfolio is invested in non‑GBP assets, exposing you to exchange‑rate movements.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no protection of your capital; losses are possible.  
- **Liquidity:** While withdrawals can be made daily and are usually paid within five business days, the ISA is not flexible – any amount withdrawn cannot be replaced in the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.  
- **Minimum holding period:** The fund recommends a minimum holding period of five years; exiting earlier may expose you to higher short‑term volatility.
