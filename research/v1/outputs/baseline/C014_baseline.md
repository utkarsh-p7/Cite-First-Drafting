## The advice we are giving you  
We recommend that you invest a lump‑sum of £20,500 in a Stocks & Shares ISA (the “ss_isa”) holding the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund. The investment will be made within the ISA wrapper, which provides tax‑free growth on income and capital gains.

## Your objectives, circumstances, knowledge and experience  
- **Primary objective:** grow long‑term savings beyond cash.  
- **Time horizon:** around 5 years.  
- **Risk tolerance:** moderately adventurous (ATR score 4). You are comfortable with meaningful swings in value.  
- **Financial situation:**  
  - Gross annual income £35,300 (salary, self‑employed).  
  - Essential monthly expenditure £1,500; discretionary monthly expenditure £1,100.  
  - Cash savings £8,000; no existing investments or liabilities.  
  - Emergency fund covers about 5.3 months of expenses.  
  - Capacity for loss is assessed as “medium”, based on a stable income, adequate emergency fund and a moderate surplus.  
- **Knowledge and experience:** you have previously held cash savings, funds and shares, but trade only rarely.  

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  
The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is a diversified multi‑asset portfolio (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds) whose investment objective is capital growth over a period of five years or more. This matches your 5‑year horizon and desire for growth beyond cash.  

The fund’s risk indicator is 4 of 7, aligning with your moderately adventurous risk profile and your expressed comfort with meaningful market swings. Your medium capacity for loss, supported by a stable income and an emergency fund of over five months, is consistent with the level of market risk inherent in the fund.  

Holding the investment inside a Stocks & Shares ISA means any income or capital gains are free from UK income tax and capital gains tax, which is appropriate for a basic‑rate taxpayer seeking tax‑efficient growth. The ISA also allows daily dealing and withdrawals within five business days, giving you flexibility should you need access before the recommended minimum holding period of five years.

## Risks and possible disadvantages  
- **Market risk:** the value of the fund can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** there is no protection of your capital; losses are possible.  
- **Minimum holding period:** the fund is recommended for at least five years; withdrawing earlier may affect the achievement of your growth objective.  
- **ISA withdrawal rules:** once you withdraw funds, you cannot replace them within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.
