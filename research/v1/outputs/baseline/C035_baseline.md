## Why this recommendation is suitable for you

The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is a diversified multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds) that seeks capital growth over a period of five years or more. This aligns with your objective of growing savings over a 10‑year horizon.  

Your high capacity for loss and existing liquid assets mean you can tolerate the market risk associated with a fund that has a risk indicator of 4 out of 7. The fund’s policy of no capital guarantee and its exposure to equities and bonds matches the growth‑oriented profile you are seeking.  

Holding the investment in a General Investment Account (GIA) is appropriate because you have already used part of your ISA allowance (£2,000) and the GIA has no subscription limit, allowing you to invest the full £29,000 in one lump sum. The GIA also provides daily dealing and unrestricted withdrawals, giving you flexibility should your circumstances change.

## Risks and possible disadvantages

- **Market risk:** The value of the fund can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no protection of your capital; losses are possible.  
- **Tax treatment:** As the GIA is not tax‑sheltered, dividends are taxable above the dividend allowance and any capital gains are subject to CGT above the annual exempt amount.
