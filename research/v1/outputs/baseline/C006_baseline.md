## The advice we are giving you  

We recommend that you invest the £29,000 inheritance as a lump‑sum into a Stocks & Shares ISA (ss_isa). The ISA will hold the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund, a diversified multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds).  

## Your objectives, circumstances, knowledge and experience  

- **Objective:** You want to invest an inheritance for growth over a horizon of around 20 years.  
- **Age & employment:** 65 years old and currently employed.  
- **Financial situation:** Gross annual income of £104,800 from salary; essential monthly expenditure £900 and discretionary monthly expenditure £700. You have £38,000 cash, no existing investments, no liabilities and an emergency fund covering over 42 months. Your capacity for loss is described as “high” because of substantial liquid assets and high disposable income relative to commitments.  
- **Tax position:** You are in the higher rate tax band.  
- **Knowledge & experience:** You have previously held cash savings and a Stocks & Shares ISA, but you have never traded.  

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

- **Investment term:** The fund is designed for capital growth over a period of 5 years or more, matching your 20‑year horizon.  
- **Risk profile:** The fund carries a risk indicator of 4 / 7 (moderate). Your stated attitude to risk is cautious (ATR score 1) and you indicated you do not want to see the value fall noticeably. While the fund can fall in value, its diversified multi‑asset composition aims to smooth volatility, which is consistent with a cautious investor who nevertheless has a high capacity for loss.  
- **Capacity for loss:** Your high capacity for loss, substantial liquid assets and high disposable income support the suitability of a moderate‑risk, growth‑oriented investment.  
- **Tax efficiency:** As the investment is held within a Stocks & Shares ISA, any income and capital gains are free from UK income tax and CGT, which is advantageous given your higher‑rate tax position.  
- **Liquidity:** Daily dealing and withdrawals within five business days give you access to the money if needed, while the ISA wrapper protects the tax‑advantaged status of the investment.  

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

- **Market risk:** The value of the fund can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back the £29,000 you invest.  
- **ISA restrictions:** The ISA is not flexible; any amount withdrawn cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.
