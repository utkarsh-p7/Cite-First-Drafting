## The advice we are giving you

We recommend that you invest a lump‑sum of £20,500 in a Stocks & Shares ISA (SS ISA). The ISA will hold the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund, a diversified multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds). This investment is intended to help you grow your long‑term savings beyond cash over a horizon of around 10 years.

- **Primary objective:** Grow long‑term savings beyond cash.  
- **Target amount:** £132,000.  
- **Investment horizon:** Approximately 10 years.  
- **Risk tolerance:** Moderately adventurous (ATR score 4). You are comfortable with meaningful swings in value.  
- **Financial situation:**  
  - Gross annual income: £22,700 (salary).  
  - Monthly essential expenditure: £1,300.  
  - Monthly discretionary expenditure: £500.  
  - Cash assets: £33,500; no existing investments or liabilities.  
  - Emergency fund covers about 25.8 months of expenses.  
  - Capacity for loss: Medium, based on stable income, adequate emergency fund and a moderate surplus.  
- **Knowledge and experience:**  
  - Previously held only cash savings.  
  - Trade rarely.  
  - No professional or relevant occupational experience in investing.

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you

The SS ISA holding the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund matches your moderately adventurous risk profile (risk indicator 4 of 7) and your desire for capital growth over a 5‑plus‑year period. The fund’s diversified multi‑asset composition aligns with your objective to grow savings beyond cash while accepting market and currency risk. The ISA wrapper provides tax‑free treatment of income and gains, supporting your long‑term target of £132,000. Your medium capacity for loss, stable income and substantial emergency fund mean you can tolerate the potential short‑term fluctuations of this investment.

## Risks and possible disadvantages

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose you to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back your original investment.  
- **Liquidity:** While daily dealing is available, withdrawals are not replaceable within the same tax year because the ISA is not flexible.  
- **Minimum holding period:** The fund recommends a minimum holding period of 5 years; exiting earlier may expose you to higher volatility.

## Costs and charges

- No entry, exit or performance fees.  
- Illustrative total annual cost: 0.85 % of the investment value.

Given the 10‑year horizon and the medium capacity for loss, a periodic review of the investment is likely to be needed to ensure it remains appropriate as your circumstances, objectives or market conditions change.
