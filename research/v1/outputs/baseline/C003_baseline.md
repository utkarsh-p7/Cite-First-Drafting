## Why this recommendation is suitable for you

The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is a diversified multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds) that aims for capital growth over a period of five years or more. Its risk indicator is 4 of 7, matching your moderately adventurous risk profile.  

Your primary goal is to build a fund for future university costs, with no fixed horizon, and you have expressed comfort with market fluctuations. The fund’s investment policy of seeking growth aligns with that objective.  

Your high capacity for loss and substantial liquid assets mean you can absorb potential short‑term declines without affecting your day‑to‑day finances. The GIA wrapper is appropriate because you have already used part of your ISA allowance and the investment is not required to be tax‑sheltered for your stated purpose.

## Risks and possible disadvantages

- **Market risk:** The value of the fund can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Holdings outside the UK are exposed to foreign‑exchange movements, which can affect returns.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no protection of your capital; you may lose part or all of the investment.  
- **Longer holding period recommended:** The fund suggests a minimum holding period of five years to smooth out volatility.  
- **Tax treatment:** As the GIA is not tax‑sheltered, dividends are taxable above the dividend allowance and any capital gains are subject to CGT above the annual exempt amount.
