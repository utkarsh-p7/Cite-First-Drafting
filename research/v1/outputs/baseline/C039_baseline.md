## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is designed to achieve capital growth over a period of five years or more, matching your stated investment horizon. Its diversified multi‑asset composition (equities and bonds) aligns with a moderately cautious risk profile while still offering the potential for higher returns than cash, which meets your primary objective of growing savings beyond cash.  

The fund’s risk indicator is 4 / 7, reflecting a level of risk that is consistent with your stated capacity for loss (medium) and your willingness to accept some volatility. The recommended minimum holding period of five years matches the time‑frame you have in mind.  

Because the GIA is an unwrapped (non‑tax‑sheltered) wrapper, any dividends and capital gains will be subject to the usual UK tax rules, which is appropriate for a client in the basic tax band and with no ISA allowance already used.  

The platform fee (0.25 % p.a.) and the fund’s ongoing charge (0.60 % p.a.) give a total illustrative cost of 0.85 % per year, which is transparent and competitive for a retail multi‑asset fund.  

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

- **Market risk:** The value of the fund can fall below the amount you invest, especially in a poor market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Part of the portfolio is invested in non‑GBP assets, exposing you to exchange‑rate movements.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back your original capital.  
- **Tax treatment:** As a non‑tax‑sheltered account, dividends and gains are taxable above the personal allowances, which could reduce net returns.
