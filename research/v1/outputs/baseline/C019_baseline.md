## The advice we are giving you  

We recommend that you invest the £17,000 you have available for a long‑term growth portfolio in the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund.  
To make the most of the tax advantages that are available to you, we suggest using **both** a Stocks & Shares ISA wrapper and an unwrapped General Investment Account (GIA) wrapper. The fund will be held in the same retail share class in each wrapper, and the investment will be made on a regular monthly basis.

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

* **Alignment with objectives and horizon:** The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is designed for capital growth over a period of five years or more, matching your 10‑year outlook.  
* **Risk profile match:** The fund carries a risk indicator of 4 / 7, which is consistent with an adventurous risk tolerance and a medium capacity for loss.  
* **Tax efficiency:** Using a Stocks & Shares ISA shelters income and capital gains from UK tax, while the GIA allows you to invest any amount beyond the remaining ISA allowance. Splitting the investment between the two wrappers therefore maximises tax efficiency while still giving you full exposure to the fund.  
* **Cost considerations:** Both wrappers incur the same platform fee (0.25 % p.a.) and fund ongoing charge (0.60 % p.a.), giving a total illustrative charge of 0.85 % p.a., which is reasonable for a diversified multi‑asset fund.  
* **Liquidity and access:** Daily dealing and withdrawals within five business days suit your need for flexibility, while the recommended minimum holding period of five years aligns with your long‑term goal.  

---

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

* **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
* **Currency risk:** A portion of the portfolio is invested in non‑GBP assets, exposing you to exchange‑rate movements.  
* **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back the original capital.  
* **ISA withdrawal restrictions:** If you withdraw from the ISA, the amount cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.
