We recommend that you invest £28,500 of your cash savings into a Stocks & Shares ISA (SS ISA) and make regular monthly contributions into the same wrapper. ## Why this recommendation is suitable for you

The SS ISA with the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund matches your balanced risk profile (risk indicator 4 of 7) and your long‑term horizon of around 20 years, giving the fund time to recover from any market downturns. The fund’s objective of capital growth over 5 + years aligns with your aim to grow the deposit over the next two decades.  

Because the ISA wrapper shelters income and capital gains from UK tax, it helps you retain more of the investment growth, supporting your target house‑deposit amount. The minimum investment (£1,000 lump sum or £50 per month) fits your available investable amount of £28,500 and allows you to spread contributions monthly, matching your cash‑flow situation.

## Risks and possible disadvantages

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose you to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no protection of your capital; losses are possible.  
- **Liquidity:** While you can withdraw daily, the ISA is not flexible – withdrawn amounts cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.  
- **Holding period:** The product is recommended for a minimum of 5 years; exiting earlier may reduce the chance of achieving your growth target.
