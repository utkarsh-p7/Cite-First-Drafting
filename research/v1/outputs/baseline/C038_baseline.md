## The advice we are giving you

We recommend that you invest the £34,500 inheritance as a lump‑sum into a Stocks & Shares ISA (SS ISA). The ISA will hold the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund, a diversified multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds). This recommendation is made in line with your objective to grow the inheritance over a roughly 10‑year horizon.

- **Financial situation:**  
  - Gross annual income: £101,100 (salary).

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you

The Stocks & Shares ISA wrapper provides tax‑free growth, matching your higher‑rate tax position. The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund aims for capital growth over a 5‑plus‑year period, aligning with your 10‑year horizon. Its risk indicator of 4 / 7 corresponds to your moderately adventurous risk profile. The fund’s diversified multi‑asset composition (equities and bonds) is appropriate for a medium capacity for loss, and the recommended minimum holding period of 5 years fits within your longer‑term plan. The ISA’s daily dealing and ability to withdraw (subject to ISA rules) give you flexibility while preserving the tax advantages.

## Risks and possible disadvantages

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose you to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back your original investment.  
- **ISA withdrawal rules:** The ISA is not flexible; amounts withdrawn cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.  
- **Liquidity:** While withdrawals are processed within five business days, the fund’s underlying assets may be less liquid than cash.
