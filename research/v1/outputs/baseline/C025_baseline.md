## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  
- **Investment term:** the ISA’s investment objective is capital growth over a period of five years or more, matching your 15‑year horizon.  
- **Risk profile:** you indicated you are “happy to take significant risk for higher returns” and your risk tolerance score is 5 (adventurous). The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund carries a risk indicator of 4 / 7, which is consistent with an adventurous but not extreme risk appetite.  
- **Capacity for loss:** your medium capacity for loss is supported by a stable salary, an emergency fund of more than three months, and a modest surplus, making the potential volatility of a multi‑asset fund acceptable.  
- **Tax efficiency:** as a Stocks & Shares ISA, any income or capital gains generated within the fund are free from UK income tax and capital gains tax, which aligns with your desire to maximise growth from the inheritance.  
- **Liquidity:** the ISA allows daily dealing and withdrawals are normally processed within five business days, giving you access to the funds if needed, while still encouraging a long‑term hold.  
- **Affordability:** the minimum investment of £1,000 lump sum or £50 per month fits your ability to make regular monthly contributions from your surplus.  

## Risks and possible disadvantages  
- **Market risk:** the value of the fund can fall below the amount invested, especially in years when equity markets perform poorly.  
- **Currency risk:** a portion of the fund is invested in non‑GBP assets, exposing you to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** there is no guarantee that you will receive back the original £14,500.  
- **ISA flexibility:** because this is not a flexible ISA, any amount you withdraw cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your remaining ISA allowance.  
- **Holding period:** the product literature recommends a minimum holding period of five years; exiting earlier may increase the likelihood of experiencing a loss.
