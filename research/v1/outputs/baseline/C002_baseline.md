## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

- **Alignment with objective and horizon:** The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is designed for capital growth over a period of five years or more, matching your 10‑year target for a house deposit.  
- **Risk profile:** The fund carries a risk indicator of 4 / 7, which is consistent with a balanced risk appetite. You have indicated that occasional market dips are acceptable.  
- **Tax efficiency:** Using a Stocks & Shares ISA shelters any income and capital gains from UK tax, helping you retain more of the investment growth. Because you have already used part of your ISA allowance, the remaining allowance can be used for the new investment.  
- **Flexibility:** The GIA component provides unrestricted access to your money, allowing withdrawals at any time without the ISA’s “no‑replace‑within‑tax‑year” rule. This dual‑wrapper approach balances tax efficiency with liquidity.  
- **Cost‑effectiveness:** Both wrappers use the same platform and fund, with total annual charges of approximately 0.85 % (0.25 % platform fee + 0.60 % fund OCF), and there are no entry, exit or performance fees.  
- **Suitability of contribution method:** Your preference for regular monthly contributions can be met in either wrapper (minimum £50 per month), supporting disciplined saving towards your deposit goal.  

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** A portion of the portfolio is invested in non‑GBP assets, exposing you to exchange‑rate movements.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back the original £7,500 plus contributions.  
- **ISA withdrawal restrictions:** Money withdrawn from the ISA cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance again.  
- **Potential tax on GIA:** Gains and dividends in the GIA are subject to UK CGT and dividend tax above the relevant allowances.
