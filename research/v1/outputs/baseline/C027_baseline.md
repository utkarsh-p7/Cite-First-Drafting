## Why this recommendation is suitable for you
The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is a diversified multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds) whose investment objective is capital growth over a period of five years or more. This matches your 7‑year horizon and your desire for growth beyond cash.  

The fund’s risk indicator is 4 of 7, aligning with your moderately adventurous risk profile and your expressed comfort with market swings. The ISA wrapper provides tax‑free treatment of income and capital gains, which is appropriate for a basic‑rate taxpayer like yourself.  

Your medium capacity for loss, stable employment and substantial emergency fund mean you can tolerate the market risk and currency risk inherent in the fund, while still preserving the ability to meet day‑to‑day financial commitments.

## Risks and possible disadvantages
- **Market risk:** The value of the fund can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back the amount invested.  
- **Liquidity:** Although you can deal daily, withdrawals are not flexible within the same tax year – amounts withdrawn cannot be replaced without using your ISA allowance.  
- **Minimum holding period:** The fund is recommended for a minimum of five years; exiting earlier may increase the chance of loss.
