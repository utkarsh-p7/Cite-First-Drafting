## The advice we are giving you  

We recommend that you invest the £34,500 you have available as a lump‑sum into the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund.  To make the most of tax efficiency and flexibility you should place part of the investment in a Stocks & Shares ISA and the remainder in an unwrapped General Investment Account (GIA).  This is the “both” wrapper recommendation recorded in your fact‑find.

* **Primary objective:** build a house deposit.

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

* **Alignment with objective and horizon:** The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is designed for capital growth over a period of five years or more, matching your 20‑year goal of saving for a house deposit.  
* **Risk profile match:** The fund carries a risk indicator of 4 / 7, reflecting a balanced multi‑asset approach (approximately 60 % global equities, 40 % bonds). This sits comfortably within an adventurous risk tolerance and your high capacity for loss.  
* **Tax efficiency:** Using a Stocks & Shares ISA shelters any income and capital gains from UK tax, which is beneficial given your higher‑rate tax band. The GIA component provides flexibility for any amount that exceeds the ISA allowance or for future additional contributions.  
* **Cost considerations:** Both the ISA and GIA wrappers incur the same platform fee (0.25 % p.a.) and fund ongoing charge (0.60 % p.a.), giving a total illustrative charge of 0.85 % p.a., which is reasonable for a diversified multi‑asset fund.  
* **Liquidity and access:** Daily dealing and withdrawals within five business days suit your need for flexibility over a long horizon, while the recommended minimum holding period of five years aligns with your 20‑year plan.  

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

* **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a market downturn.  
* **Currency risk:** A portion of the portfolio is invested in non‑GBP assets, exposing you to exchange‑rate movements.  
* **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back your original capital.  
* **ISA withdrawal restrictions:** Once you withdraw from the ISA you cannot replace the amount in the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.
