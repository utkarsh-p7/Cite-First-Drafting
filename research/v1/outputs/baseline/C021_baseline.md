## The advice we are giving you  

We recommend that you invest the £39,500 you have available as a lump‑sum into an unwrapped General Investment Account (GIA). The GIA will hold the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund (a multi‑asset fund with roughly 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds).  

## Your objectives, circumstances, knowledge and experience  

* **Objective** – You want to grow your wealth generally and have indicated a time‑frame of around three years.  
* **Age & employment** – You are 59 years old and self‑employed, earning a gross annual income of £140,700.  
* **Financial situation** –  
  * Monthly essential outgoings: £3,450  
  * Monthly discretionary outgoings: £1,000  
  * Cash savings: £37,000  
  * Existing investments: £63,000  
  * No liabilities and an emergency‑fund buffer of about 10.7 months.  
* **Capacity for loss** – You have a high capacity for loss, based on substantial liquid assets and high disposable income relative to your commitments.  
* **Knowledge & experience** – You have not held investment products before and only trade “sometimes”.  

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

* **Risk tolerance** – You described yourself as “adventurous” (ATR score 5) and are happy to take significant risk for higher returns. The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund carries a risk indicator of 4 / 7, which aligns with an adventurous risk appetite.  
* **Investment horizon** – Although the fund’s policy suggests a minimum holding period of five years, you are comfortable with a three‑year horizon and understand that the fund may experience short‑term volatility. Your high capacity for loss means a temporary fall in value would not affect your day‑to‑day finances.  
* **Liquidity** – The GIA offers daily dealing and withdrawals are normally paid within five business days, giving you the flexibility to access the money if needed before the five‑year horizon.  
* **No entry or exit charges** – The product has no front‑end or back‑end fees, which suits a lump‑sum investment of £39,500.  

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

* **Market risk** – The fund’s value can fall below the amount you invest, especially in a bad market year.  
* **Currency risk** – Part of the portfolio is invested in non‑GBP assets, exposing you to exchange‑rate movements.  
* **No capital guarantee** – There is no guarantee that you will receive back your original investment.  
* **Recommended holding period** – The fund is designed for a five‑year or longer term; investing for only three years may mean you experience higher volatility and potentially lower returns than the long‑term objective of capital growth.  

## Costs and charges

* Fund ongoing charge (OCF): 0.60 % per annum  
* No entry, exit or performance fees  

Total illustrative annual cost: **0.85 %** of the investment value.  

## Ongoing review of your arrangements  

Because your investment horizon is shorter than the fund’s recommended minimum holding period, and because your risk appetite is high, we anticipate that a periodic review (at least annually) will be appropriate to ensure the investment remains consistent with your objectives and any change in circumstances.
