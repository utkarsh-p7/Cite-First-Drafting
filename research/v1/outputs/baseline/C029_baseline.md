## The advice we are giving you

We recommend that you invest £9,500 in a Stocks & Shares ISA (SS ISA) and hold the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund within that ISA. The investment may be made as a lump‑sum contribution (the “both” contribution type indicated in the fact‑find).

## Your objectives, circumstances, knowledge and experience

- **Primary objective:** Save for your children’s university costs.  
- **Time horizon:** Approximately five years, possibly extending to ten years.  
- **Risk tolerance:** You describe yourself as “adventurous” (ATR score 5) and are happy to take significant risk for higher returns.  
- **Financial situation:**  
  - Gross annual income £30,500 (salary).  
  - Monthly essential expenditure £1,550 and discretionary expenditure £550.  
  - Cash savings £20,500 and existing investments £74,000.  
  - Liabilities £15,500, giving an emergency fund of about 13 months (well above the typical three‑month recommendation).  
  - Capacity for loss is “medium”, based on a stable income, adequate emergency fund and a moderate surplus.  
- **Knowledge and experience:** You have previously held cash savings, funds and shares, and trade only rarely. You have no professional background in finance.

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you

The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is a multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities, 40 % bonds) whose investment objective is capital growth over a period of five years or more. This matches your stated horizon of “five‑ish years, could be ten”. The fund’s risk indicator is 4 / 7, reflecting a moderate‑to‑high level of market risk, which aligns with your adventurous risk attitude and willingness to accept significant fluctuations for higher potential returns.

Holding the fund inside a Stocks & Shares ISA provides tax‑free treatment of any income and capital gains, supporting your goal of maximising the amount available for university costs. The ISA wrapper also allows daily dealing and relatively quick access to funds (withdrawals normally paid within five business days), giving you flexibility should your circumstances change.

Your capacity for loss is assessed as medium, and the combination of a stable salary, a strong emergency fund and a moderate surplus means you are able to absorb potential short‑term declines in the fund’s value without jeopardising day‑to‑day finances.

## Risks and possible disadvantages

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back the original £9,500.  
- **Minimum holding period:** The product is recommended for a minimum of five years; exiting earlier may increase the chance of loss.  
- **ISA limitations:** Once you withdraw from this non‑flexible ISA, the withdrawn amount cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.

## Costs and charges

- Fund ongoing charge (OCF): 0.60 % per annum.

The illustrative total annual cost is therefore 0.85 % of the investment value.

Given the medium‑term horizon and the possibility that your children’s university dates or your personal circumstances may change, a periodic review (at least annually) is likely to be needed to ensure the investment remains appropriate for your objectives and risk tolerance.
