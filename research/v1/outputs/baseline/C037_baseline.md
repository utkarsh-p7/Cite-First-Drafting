## The advice we are giving you  

We recommend that you invest the £35,000 you have available in a balanced multi‑asset fund – the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund – using two wrappers: a Stocks & Shares ISA and an unwrapped General Investment Account (GIA). The fund will be held in the same retail share class in each wrapper and will be funded on a regular monthly basis.

## Your objectives, circumstances, knowledge and experience  

* **Primary objective:** to supplement your retirement savings outside of a pension.  
* **Time‑frame:** around 5 years.  
* **Risk tolerance:** you indicated a balanced attitude to risk (ATR score 3) and are comfortable with “some ups and downs” for the prospect of better growth.  
* **Financial situation:**  
  * Gross annual income £23,800 (salary).  
  * Essential monthly outgoings £1,100 and discretionary spending £1,000.  
  * Cash savings £31,500 and no existing investments, property or liabilities.  
  * Emergency fund covers roughly 28 months, giving you a medium capacity for loss based on a stable income, adequate emergency fund and a moderate surplus.  
* **Knowledge & experience:** you have not held investments before but trade frequently, indicating a willingness to engage with the market.  

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

* **Investment term:** the fund’s policy is to seek capital growth over a period of 5 years or more, matching your 5‑year horizon.  
* **Risk profile:** the fund carries a summary risk indicator of 4 out of 7, which aligns with your balanced risk tolerance and medium capacity for loss.  
* **Diversification:** the fund is a multi‑asset portfolio (approximately 60 % global equities, 40 % bonds) providing diversification that supports growth while moderating volatility.  
* **Tax efficiency:** using a Stocks & Shares ISA shelters income and capital gains from UK tax, helping you keep more of any growth. The GIA allows any remaining amount to be invested without the ISA allowance limit, ensuring the full £35,000 can be deployed.  
* **Liquidity:** both wrappers allow daily dealing and withdrawals are normally processed within five business days, giving you access to the money if your circumstances change.  
* **Cost‑effectiveness:** the total annual charge of 0.85 % (platform fee 0.25 % + fund OCF 0.60 %) is the same for both wrappers, providing a transparent and low‑cost structure.  

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

* **Market risk:** the fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
* **Currency risk:** a portion of the portfolio is invested in non‑GBP assets, exposing you to exchange‑rate movements.  
* **No capital guarantee:** there is no guarantee that you will receive back your original capital.  
* **ISA withdrawal rules:** amounts withdrawn from the ISA cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.  
* **Tax on GIA:** dividends and any capital gains realised in the GIA are subject to UK income tax and CGT above the relevant allowances.
