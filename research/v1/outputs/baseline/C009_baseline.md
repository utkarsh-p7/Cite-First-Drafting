## Why this recommendation is suitable for you

The Stocks & Shares ISA matches your primary objective of wealth accumulation over a medium‑term horizon (5 + years) and aligns with your high capacity for loss. The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund seeks capital growth through a diversified mix of equities and bonds, which is appropriate for a 7‑year investment period.  

The ISA wrapper provides tax‑free treatment of income and capital gains, which is beneficial given your additional‑rate tax position. The product’s risk indicator is 4 of 7, reflecting a moderate‑to‑high risk level that is consistent with your stated willingness to accept market fluctuations and your ability to absorb losses without affecting day‑to‑day finances.

## Risks and possible disadvantages

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to foreign exchange movements.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no protection of the capital you invest.  
- **Liquidity:** While you can withdraw daily, the ISA is not flexible – withdrawn amounts cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.  
- **Holding period:** The fund recommends a minimum holding period of 5 years; exiting earlier may increase the chance of loss.
