## The advice we are giving you  

We recommend that you invest the £30,000 lump‑sum you have available in the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund.  To give you tax efficiency now and flexibility later, we suggest placing part of the investment in a Stocks & Shares ISA and part in an unwrapped General Investment Account (GIA).  This “both‑wrapper” approach matches the recommendation recorded in your fact‑find.

* **Primary objective** – to supplement your retirement savings outside of a pension.  
* **Time horizon** – around three years.

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

* **Alignment with objective and horizon** – The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is a multi‑asset fund that seeks capital growth over a 5‑plus‑year period.  While the fund’s recommended minimum holding period is five years, the blend of equities and bonds provides a smoother return profile than a pure equity fund, which is more appropriate for a cautious investor with a three‑year horizon.  

* **Risk profile** – The fund carries a Summary Risk Indicator of 4 / 7, which sits in the medium‑risk range.  This is consistent with your cautious attitude and your stated desire to avoid noticeable drops, while still offering the potential for growth to supplement retirement savings.  

* **Capacity for loss** – Your high capacity for loss, demonstrated by large cash reserves and a strong disposable income, means you can absorb short‑term market fluctuations that may occur with a medium‑risk fund.  

* **Tax efficiency** – Using a Stocks & Shares ISA shelters any income and capital gains from UK tax, which is beneficial given your higher‑rate tax band.  The GIA component provides flexibility to withdraw any amount at any time without the ISA’s “no‑re‑place” rule, supporting your need for liquidity over the three‑year period.  

* **Costs** – Both wrappers use the same platform and fund charges (0.25 % platform fee + 0.60 % fund OCF = 0.85 % total per annum).  There are no entry, exit or performance fees, keeping the cost structure simple and transparent.  

* **Access and withdrawals** – Daily dealing and withdrawals within five business days suit your desire for liquidity.  The GIA allows unrestricted withdrawals, while the ISA’s withdrawals are also prompt, though any withdrawn ISA amount cannot be replaced within the same tax year.  

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

* **Market risk** – The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a market downturn.  This risk is highlighted in the product factsheet (risk indicator 4 / 7).  
* **Currency risk** – A portion of the fund is invested in non‑GBP assets, exposing you to exchange‑rate movements.  
* **No capital guarantee** – Neither the ISA nor the GIA provides a guarantee that you will receive back your original capital.  
* **Recommended holding period** – The fund’s literature suggests a minimum holding period of five years to smooth volatility, which is longer than your three‑year horizon.  This mismatch means you may experience more short‑term fluctuation than you would prefer.  
* **ISA withdrawal limitation** – If you withdraw from the ISA, you cannot replace the withdrawn amount within the same tax year without using additional ISA allowance.
