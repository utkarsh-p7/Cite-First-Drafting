## Why this recommendation is suitable for you

The SS ISA holding the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund matches your stated objectives and risk profile:

- **Investment term:** The fund’s policy is capital growth over 5 + years, aligning with your 20‑year horizon.  
- **Risk level:** The fund’s risk indicator is 4 of 7, reflecting a moderate‑to‑high risk profile that is consistent with your adventurous attitude and willingness to accept significant short‑term fluctuations.  
- **Capacity for loss:** Your medium capacity for loss, supported by a large cash buffer and stable income, is compatible with the market risk inherent in the fund.  
- **Tax efficiency:** As an ISA, any income and capital gains are free from UK income tax and CGT, which supports the long‑term growth of your career‑break fund.  
- **Liquidity:** Daily dealing and withdrawals within five business days give you flexibility should you need access before the end of the 20‑year period, though withdrawn amounts cannot be replaced within the same tax year.

## Risks and possible disadvantages

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to currency fluctuations.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back your original capital.  
- **Minimum holding period:** The product is recommended for a minimum of five years; exiting earlier may expose you to higher volatility.  
- **ISA withdrawal rules:** Once you withdraw, you cannot replace the amount within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.
