## Risks and possible disadvantages  

- **Market risk:** The value of the fund can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Part of the portfolio is invested in non‑GBP assets, exposing you to exchange‑rate movements.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back the £13,500 you invest.  
- **Liquidity:** While you can deal daily, withdrawals are not “flexible” – any amount taken out cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your ISA allowance.  
- **Holding period:** The fund recommends a minimum holding period of 5 years; selling earlier may increase the chance of a loss.
