- **Investment term & objective:** The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund aims for capital growth over a 5‑plus‑year horizon, matching your desire for growth.

## Risks and possible disadvantages

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back the £21,500 you invest.  
- **Recommended holding period:** The fund’s documentation suggests a minimum holding period of five years, which is longer than your three‑year target horizon; achieving the £101,000 target within three years is therefore not guaranteed.  
- **ISA flexibility:** Once withdrawn, the amount cannot be replaced in the same tax year without using further ISA allowance.
