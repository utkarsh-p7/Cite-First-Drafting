## The advice we are giving you  

We recommend that you invest £19,000 in a Stocks & Shares ISA (the “ss_isa”) and hold the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund within that ISA. The investment will be made as a lump‑sum contribution.

## Your objectives, circumstances, knowledge and experience  

- **Primary objective:** Save for your children's university costs.  
- **Time horizon:** Around 10 years.  
- **Risk attitude:** You described yourself as “adventurous” and are happy to take significant risk for higher returns.  
- **Capacity for loss:** High – you have substantial liquid assets and a high disposable income relative to your commitments.  
- **Financial situation:**  
  - Gross annual income £83,200 (salary).  
  - Monthly essential expenditure £3,300 and discretionary expenditure £850.  
  - Cash assets £33,000 and no existing investments or liabilities.  
  - Emergency fund covering 10 months of expenses.  
- **Knowledge and experience:** You have not held any investment products before and have never traded.  

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

The Stocks & Shares ISA holds a diversified multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds) whose investment objective is capital growth over a period of five years or more. This matches your 10‑year horizon and your willingness to accept market volatility in pursuit of higher returns.  

Your high capacity for loss and adventurous risk profile mean you can tolerate the fund’s risk indicator of 4 / 7 and the possibility that the value may fall below the amount invested.  

Using an ISA wrapper gives you tax‑free treatment on any income and capital gains generated by the fund, which is beneficial given your higher‑rate tax band. The recommended minimum investment (£1,000 lump sum) is satisfied by your £19,000 available to invest.

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall, potentially below the amount you invest, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose the investment to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back your original capital.  
- **Liquidity:** While you can withdraw daily, the ISA is not flexible – any amount withdrawn cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.  
- **Holding period:** The fund recommends a minimum holding period of five years; exiting earlier may increase the chance of loss.
