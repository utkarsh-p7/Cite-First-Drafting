## The advice we are giving you  

We recommend that you invest the £25,500 inheritance as a lump‑sum into a Stocks & Shares ISA (ss_isa). The ISA will hold the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund, a diversified multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds).  

## Your objectives, circumstances, knowledge and experience  

- **Primary objective:** invest an inheritance for growth over a horizon of around 20 years.  
- **Age:** 50, retired.  
- **Employment status:** retired, with a stable gross annual income of £88,300 (salary).  
- **Dependants:** 3.  
- **Financial situation:**  
  - Monthly essential expenditure £2,500 and discretionary expenditure £1,400.  
  - Cash savings £12,500, no existing investments, liabilities £2,500.  
  - Emergency fund covers 5 months of expenses.  
  - Capacity for loss is assessed as “medium” based on a stable income, adequate emergency fund and a moderate surplus.  
- **Tax position:** higher‑rate taxpayer. No ISA allowance has been used this year.  
- **Knowledge & experience:** no previous investment products held, trades rarely, no professional background in finance.  

## Why this recommendation is suitable for you  

- **Investment term:** the fund is designed for capital growth over a period of 5 years or more, matching your 20‑year horizon.  
- **Risk profile:** the fund carries a risk indicator of 4 / 7 (moderate risk). This aligns with your cautious attitude to risk (ATR score 1) and your stated desire not to see large drops in value, while still offering growth potential over the long term.  
- **Capacity for loss:** your medium capacity for loss, supported by a stable income and an emergency fund of five months, is compatible with the moderate market risk of the fund.  
- **Tax efficiency:** as a Stocks & Shares ISA, any income and capital gains generated are free from UK income tax and CGT, which is beneficial given your higher‑rate tax band.  
- **Liquidity:** the ISA allows daily dealing and withdrawals within five business days, providing access to funds if needed, though withdrawn amounts cannot be replaced within the same tax year.  
- **Cost‑effectiveness:** total annual charges of 0.85 % (platform fee 0.25 % + fund OCF 0.60 %) are transparent and there are no entry, exit or performance fees.  

## Risks and possible disadvantages  

- **Market risk:** the value of the fund can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year.  
- **Currency risk:** holdings outside the UK are exposed to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates.  
- **No capital guarantee:** there is no guarantee that you will receive back the original £25,500.  
- **Minimum holding period:** the fund recommends a minimum of five years to help smooth short‑term volatility.  
- **ISA flexibility:** once you withdraw money, you cannot replace it in the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance.  

## Costs and charges  

- Platform fee: 0.25 % per annum.  
- Fund ongoing charge (OCF): 0.60 % per annum.  
- No entry, exit or performance fees.  
- Illustrative total annual cost: 0.85 % of the investment value.  

## Ongoing review of your arrangements  

Given the 20‑year investment horizon and the moderate risk profile of the fund, a periodic review (at least annually) is likely to be needed to ensure the investment remains appropriate as your circumstances, market conditions and tax rules evolve.
