[KB:COBS_9A_3:01_applies_to_scope] (COBS_9A_3.md) Applies to (scope):
MiFID/equivalent business and IBIPs involving investment advice or portfolio management to a **retail client**. S&S ISA + GIA holding MiFID financial instruments → this chapter is the **primary** authority for v1.
[KB:COBS_9A_3:02_what_the_report_must_contain_do_paraphra] (COBS_9A_3.md) What the report must contain / do (paraphrase):
- On giving investment advice to a retail client, the firm must, **before the transaction concludes**, give a suitability report in a durable medium that states the advice given and how it fits the client's preferences, objectives and other characteristics. [Note: art 25(6) MiFID]
- MiFID Org Reg art 54(12): the report must outline the advice and why the recommendation is suitable — referencing investment term, the client's knowledge & experience, attitude to risk and **capacity for loss** — and flag whether the recommendation will likely need periodic review.
- Distance-communication carve-out: report may follow immediately after conclusion if the client consented to delayed receipt **and** was offered the option to delay the transaction to receive it first.
[KB:COBS_9A_2:01_applies_to_scope] (COBS_9A_2.md) Applies to (scope):
Suitability assessment for MiFID/IBIP investment advice or portfolio management to retail clients. Governs the **client-information substrate** the report must evidence.
[KB:COBS_9A_2:02_what_the_firm_must_do_paraphrase] (COBS_9A_2.md) What the firm must do (paraphrase):
- Obtain the information needed on the client's (a) **knowledge & experience** in the relevant field, (b) **financial situation including ability to bear losses**, (c) **investment objectives including risk tolerance** — so as to recommend only what is suitable, in particular consistent with the client's risk tolerance and ability to bear losses. [Note: art 25(2) MiFID]
- Financial-situation information must, where relevant, cover source and extent of regular income, assets (including liquid assets, investments, real property) and regular financial commitments.
- Suitability must be assessed for **all decisions to trade — including hold and sell — not only buy** recommendations.
- A bundled package recommended under COBS 6.1ZA.16R must be suitable **as a whole**.
- For a legal person / group of natural persons: establish, record and implement a policy on who is assessed and how, including from whom the information is collected.

[KB:PRIN_2A:01_applies_to_scope] (PRIN_2A.md) Applies to (scope):
All retail-market business with retail customers. In force 31/07/2023 (open products) / 31/07/2024 (closed). For investments, applies to clients who are **not professional clients**. Replaces Principles 6 & 7 where it applies; higher/more exacting standard (PRIN 2A.1.17G).
[KB:PRIN_2A:02_what_the_firm_must_do_paraphrase] (PRIN_2A.md) What the firm must do (paraphrase):
- **Principle 12:** act to deliver good outcomes for retail customers.
- **Cross-cutting rules (PRIN 2A.2):** (i) act in good faith (PRIN 2A.2.1R); (ii) avoid causing foreseeable harm (PRIN 2A.2.8R); (iii) enable and support retail customers to pursue their financial objectives (PRIN 2A.2.14)
- **Four outcomes:** products & services governance (PRIN 2A.3); **price & value / fair value** (PRIN 2A.4); **consumer understanding** (PRIN 2A.5); consumer support (PRIN 2A.6).
- Underpinned by an objective **reasonableness** test, proportionate to the firm's size and role.
