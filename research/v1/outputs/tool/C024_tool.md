## Why this recommendation is suitable for you
The SS ISA wrapper allows you to invest the £18,500 lump sum in a diversified multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds) that aims for capital growth over a period of five years or more [FS:ISA:01][FS:ISA:02]. This aligns with your 20‑year horizon and primary objective of building a sizeable university fund.  

Your moderate‑adventurous risk attitude and high capacity for loss mean you are comfortable with the fund’s risk level (summary risk indicator 4 of 7) and the possibility that the investment value may fall in a bad year [FS:ISA:03][FF:C024:objectives.atr_score][FF:C024:financial_situation.capacity_for_loss].  

The ISA wrapper provides tax‑free growth – any income or capital gains generated are free from UK income tax and CGT, which supports your long‑term savings goal [FS:ISA:05].  

The minimum investment requirement of £1,000 lump sum is met, and the fund’s charges (total illustrative cost 0.85 % p.a.) are transparent and competitive [FS:ISA:04][FS:ISA:07].

## Risks and possible disadvantages
- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a market downturn [FS:ISA:03].
