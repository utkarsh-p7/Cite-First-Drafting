## Why this recommendation is suitable for you
The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is a multi‑asset fund (approximately 60 % global equities, 40 % bonds) aiming for capital growth over a 5‑plus‑year horizon [FS:GIA:01][FS:GIA:02]. Although the fund’s policy targets a longer term, its risk indicator of 4 out of 7 aligns with an adventurous risk appetite while still being within the range you have expressed [FS:GIA:03]. The fund’s diversified nature matches your desire for wealth accumulation and your capacity to absorb market fluctuations [TR:C021:0006][TR:C021:0008].  

The GIA wrapper is unwrapped, meaning there is no tax shelter, but it offers daily dealing and unrestricted withdrawals, giving you flexibility should your circumstances change before the 5‑year recommended holding period [FS:GIA:06][FS:GIA:07]. The minimum investment requirement of £1,000 is well below your available amount, so the full £39,500 can be invested immediately [FS:GIA:07].

## Risks and possible disadvantages
- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, and there is no capital guarantee [FS:GIA:03].  
- **Currency risk:** Exposure to non‑GBP holdings may cause additional volatility [FS:GIA:03].  
- **Time horizon mismatch:** The fund’s policy recommends a minimum holding period of 5 years, whereas your stated horizon is around 3 years [FS:GIA:03][FF:C021:objectives.horizon_years].  
- **Tax treatment:** As the GIA is not tax‑sheltered, dividends are taxable above the dividend allowance and any gains are subject to Capital Gains Tax above the annual exempt amount [FS:GIA:05].
