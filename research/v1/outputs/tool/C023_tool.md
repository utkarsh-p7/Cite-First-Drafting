## Why this recommendation is suitable for you
The SS ISA holds the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund, a diversified multi‑asset portfolio (approximately 60 % global equities and 40 % bonds) that aims for capital growth over a period of five years or more, matching your 20‑year investment horizon [FS:ISA:01][FS:ISA:02]. The fund’s risk indicator of 4 / 7 aligns with your moderately adventurous risk attitude [FS:ISA:03].  

Because the ISA wrapper shelters income and capital gains from UK income tax and CGT, it helps you retain more of any growth towards your £47,000 house‑deposit goal [FS:ISA:05]. The platform fee (0.25 % p.a.) and fund OCF (0.60 % p.a.) give an illustrative total charge of 0.85 % p.a., which is reasonable for a diversified fund and consistent with delivering good outcomes for retail customers [FS:ISA:04][KB:PRIN_2A:02_what_the_firm_must_do_paraphrase].  

Your medium capacity for loss, supported by a solid emergency fund and stable self‑employment income, means you can tolerate the market and currency risks inherent in the fund, as you indicated you are comfortable with “meaningful swings” [TR:C023:0006][FF:C023:financial_situation.capacity_for_loss].


## Risks and possible disadvantages
- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year [FS:ISA:03].  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose you to exchange‑rate movements [FS:ISA:03].  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back your original capital [FS:ISA:03].  
- **Liquidity:** While daily dealing is available, withdrawals are not replaceable within the same tax year, which may limit flexibility if you need to reinvest the same amount later [FS:ISA:06].  
- **Charges:** The total annual charge of 0.85 % reduces net returns [FS:ISA:04].
