The fund’s risk indicator is 4 of 7, reflecting a moderate‑to‑high risk profile that is consistent with your stated “high” capacity for loss [FS:ISA:03][FF:C030:financial_situation.capacity_for_loss]. ## Risks and possible disadvantages
- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, and there is no capital guarantee [FS:ISA:03].  
- **Currency risk:** Exposure to non‑GBP holdings may cause additional volatility [FS:ISA:03].  
- **Liquidity constraints:** Withdrawals are processed within five business days, but once withdrawn the amount cannot be replaced in the same tax year without using your ISA allowance [FS:ISA:06].  
- **Holding period:** The product is recommended for a minimum of five years, so early exit may reduce the chance of achieving the target amount [FS:ISA:03].
