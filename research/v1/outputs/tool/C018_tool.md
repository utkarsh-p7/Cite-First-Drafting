## Why this recommendation is suitable for you
The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is a multi‑asset fund (≈ 60 % global equities, 40 % bonds) that aims for capital growth over a 5‑plus‑year horizon, matching your 5‑year university‑saving goal [FS:ISA:01][FS:GIA:01][FS:ISA:02][FS:GIA:02]. Its risk indicator of 4 out of 7 aligns with your moderately adventurous risk attitude [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03] and, given your high capacity for loss, you can tolerate the market swings you described [TR:C018:0006][FF:C018:objectives.atr_score][FF:C018:financial_situation.capacity_for_loss].  

Using a **Stocks & Shares ISA** provides tax‑free growth on income and capital gains, which is beneficial given your additional‑rate tax band [FF:C018:client.tax_band][FS:ISA:05]. The **GIA** component gives you flexibility to withdraw any amount without the ISA’s restriction on re‑placing withdrawals within the same tax year [FS:GIA:06][FS:ISA:06]. Splitting the £22,500 between the two wrappers therefore balances tax efficiency with liquidity, supporting both the growth objective and the need for possible access before the end of the 5‑year period.

## Risks and possible disadvantages
- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03].
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose you to exchange‑rate movements [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03].
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back the original £22,500 [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03].
- **Minimum holding period:** The fund is recommended to be held for at least 5 years, which matches but also limits early exit without potential loss [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03].
- **Charges:** Total annual cost of 0.85 % (platform fee 0.25 % + fund OCF 0.60 %) will reduce net returns [FS:ISA:04][FS:GIA:04].
- **ISA withdrawal limits:** Withdrawn ISA amounts cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance [FS:ISA:06].
