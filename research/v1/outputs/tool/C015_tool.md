## Why this recommendation is suitable for you
The Stocks & Shares ISA invests in a diversified multi‑asset fund (≈60 % global equities, 40 % bonds) that aims for capital growth over a period of 5 years or more, matching your 7‑year horizon [FS:ISA:01][FS:ISA:02]. Although the fund carries a medium risk rating (4 / 7) [FS:ISA:03], the potential for growth aligns with your goal of funding university costs, while the ISA wrapper provides tax‑free growth, which is beneficial given your higher‑rate tax position [FS:ISA:05]. The product has no entry, exit or performance fees and a total annual charge of 0.85 % [FS:ISA:04], which is reasonable for a retail client seeking value for money [KB:PRIN_2A:02_what_the_firm_must_do_paraphrase].
