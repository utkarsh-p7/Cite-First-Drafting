## Why this recommendation is suitable for you
The Stocks & Shares ISA holds the Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund, a diversified multi‑asset portfolio (≈60 % global equities, 40 % bonds) that aims for capital growth over a period of five years or more [FS:ISA:01][FS:ISA:02]. This matches your growth‑oriented objective and 15‑year horizon.  

Your adventurous risk attitude aligns with the fund’s risk indicator of 4 out of 7, indicating a moderate‑to‑high level of market risk, which you have indicated you are comfortable taking [FS:ISA:03][TR:C025:0006].  

The ISA wrapper provides tax‑free treatment for income and capital gains, which is beneficial given your basic‑rate tax position [FS:ISA:05].  

Your medium capacity for loss, supported by a stable salary, an emergency fund of over three months and a modest surplus, means you can absorb short‑term market fluctuations while remaining on track for long‑term growth [FF:C025:financial_situation.capacity_for_loss][FF:C025:financial_situation.capacity_for_loss_basis].

## Risks and possible disadvantages
- **Market risk:** the value of the fund can fall below the amount you invest, especially in a bad market year [FS:ISA:03].  
- **Currency risk:** non‑GBP holdings expose you to exchange‑rate movements [FS:ISA:03].  
- **No capital guarantee:** you may not get back the full amount invested [FS:ISA:03].  
- **Liquidity constraints:** withdrawals are processed within five business days, and once withdrawn you cannot replace the amount in the same tax year without using your ISA allowance [FS:ISA:06].  
- **Minimum holding period:** the product is designed for a minimum of five years, shorter periods increase the chance of loss [FS:ISA:03].
