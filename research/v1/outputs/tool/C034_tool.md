## Why this recommendation is suitable for you
The Stocks & Shares ISA invests in a diversified multi‑asset fund (≈60 % global equities, 40 % bonds) whose objective is capital growth over a period of five years or more [FS:ISA:01][FS:ISA:02]. This aligns with your desire for higher returns and willingness to accept significant risk [FF:C034:objectives.atr_descriptor][TR:C034:0006].  

Your medium capacity for loss is supported by a stable income, a solid emergency fund and a surplus, meaning you can absorb potential short‑term market declines [FF:C034:financial_situation.capacity_for_loss][FF:C034:financial_situation.capacity_for_loss_basis].  

The ISA wrapper provides tax‑free growth on income and capital gains, which is beneficial given your basic‑rate tax position [FS:ISA:05]. The product has a minimum investment of £1,000, so your £13,500 lump‑sum comfortably meets this requirement [FS:ISA:07].  

The total annual cost of 0.85 % (platform fee 0.25 % + fund OCF 0.60 %) is transparent and competitive for a diversified fund [FS:ISA:04].

## Risks and possible disadvantages
- **Market risk:** the fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year [FS:ISA:03].  
- **Currency risk:** non‑GBP holdings expose you to exchange‑rate movements [FS:ISA:03].  
- **No capital guarantee:** you may lose part or all of the invested capital [FS:ISA:03].  
- **Holding period:** the fund is intended for a minimum of five years to smooth volatility, which may not match an uncertain “until we need it” horizon [FS:ISA:03].  
- **Liquidity:** withdrawals are processed within five business days, but once withdrawn you cannot replace the amount in the same tax year without using your ISA allowance [FS:ISA:06].
