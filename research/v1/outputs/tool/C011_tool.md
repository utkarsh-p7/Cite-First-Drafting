## Why this recommendation is suitable for you
- The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund aims for capital growth over a 5‑plus‑year horizon, matching your 15‑year objective. [FS:ISA:02][FS:GIA:02]  
- The fund’s risk indicator is 4 of 7, which is consistent with a cautious attitude but still offers growth potential appropriate to your medium capacity for loss. [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03]

## Risks and possible disadvantages
- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year. [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03]  
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose you to exchange‑rate movements. [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03]  
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no protection of your capital; you could lose part of the £32,500. [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03]  
- **ISA withdrawal limits:** Withdrawn ISA amounts cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance. [FS:ISA:06]  
- **Tax on GIA:** Dividends and gains in the GIA are subject to income tax and CGT above the personal allowances. [FS:GIA:05]
