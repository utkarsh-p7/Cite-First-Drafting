## Why this recommendation is suitable for you
The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is a multi‑asset fund with an approximate 60 % equity / 40 % bond mix, matching a balanced risk profile (risk indicator 4 of 7) that aligns with your “balanced” attitude to risk [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03]​.  The fund aims for capital growth over a 5‑plus‑year horizon, which is compatible with your three‑year target because the fund’s diversified approach seeks to smooth short‑term volatility while still offering growth potential [FS:ISA:02][FS:GIA:02]​.

Holding the fund in a Stocks & Shares ISA gives you tax‑free treatment on any income and capital gains, helping you reach the £51,000 target more efficiently [FS:ISA:05]​.  The GIA wrapper provides flexibility – you can withdraw at any time without the ISA’s restriction on re‑placing withdrawn amounts within the same tax year [FS:GIA:06]​.

Your high capacity for loss, substantial liquid assets and strong disposable income mean you can tolerate the market risk inherent in a balanced fund [FF:C013:financial_situation.capacity_for_loss][FF:C013:financial_situation.capacity_for_loss_basis]​, and you have indicated you are comfortable with “some ups and downs” for better growth [TR:C013:0006]​.

## Risks and possible disadvantages
- **Market risk:** The fund’s value can fall below the amount invested, especially in a bad market year [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03]​.
- **Currency risk:** Non‑GBP holdings expose you to foreign exchange movements [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03]​.
- **No capital guarantee:** There is no guarantee that you will receive back your original investment [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03]​.
- **Recommended holding period:** The fund is intended for a minimum of five years, which exceeds your three‑year horizon; exiting earlier may mean you experience short‑term volatility [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03]​.
- **ISA withdrawal limits:** Withdrawn ISA amounts cannot be replaced within the same tax year without using your annual ISA allowance [FS:ISA:06]​.
