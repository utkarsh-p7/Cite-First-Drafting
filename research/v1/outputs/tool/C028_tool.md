## Why this recommendation is suitable for you
The Aldercroft Balanced Growth Fund is a multi‑asset portfolio (approximately 60 % global equities, 40 % bonds) that aims for capital growth over a period of five years or more [FS:ISA:01][FS:GIA:01][FS:ISA:02][FS:GIA:02]. Its risk indicator of 4 / 7 aligns with an adventurous risk appetite, offering the higher return potential you are seeking while still providing diversification [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03].  

Using a **Stocks & Shares ISA** for part of the investment gives you tax‑free growth and no capital gains tax on any gains, which is beneficial given your higher‑rate tax band [FS:ISA:05]. The **GIA** provides unrestricted access, allowing you to withdraw funds at any time without the ISA’s restriction on replacing withdrawn amounts within the same tax year [FS:GIA:06][FS:ISA:06].  

Both wrappers charge the same platform fee (0.25 % p.a.) and fund OCF (0.60 % p.a.), giving a total illustrative cost of 0.85 % per year [FS:ISA:04][FS:GIA:04]. This cost structure is transparent and consistent across the two accounts.  

Your long‑term horizon (≈20 years) comfortably exceeds the fund’s recommended minimum holding period of five years, reducing the impact of short‑term market volatility [FS:ISA:03][FS:GIA:03].
